1. **Purpose** – New concept: repeat actions with a `for` loop.
2. **Recipe List**

| Concept | Syntax / Example | Docs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| for loop | `for (int step = 1; step <= 3; step++)` | [for loop](https://learn.microsoft.com/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/statements/iteration-statements#the-for-statement) |

3. **Adventure Story / Problem** – A drumline of enchanted barrels blocks the path. They only roll aside if John beats the rhythm exactly three times with a marching loop. Use a `for` loop to print steps 1 through 3 with encouraging text.

4. **Example Input/Output**

```
Output
Step 1: left foot
Step 2: right foot
Step 3: spin!
```
