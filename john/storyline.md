# John's Labyrinth Laugh-A-Thon

## Purpose
Help John sharpen his C# fundamentals by guiding Sir Johnathan Debugsworth through a labyrinth, a groaning swamp, and an undersea realm - all while learning to wrangle strings, decisions, and loops and stuff - there will probably be chickens.  Protein is ON the menu.

## Adventure Story / Problem
Sir Johnathan Debugsworth, veteran dungeon master and part-time lawn gnome impersonator, has been hired to retrieve the Legendary Release Notes of Clarity. Unfortunately, the tome is locked behind three madcap biomes:

1. **The Labyrinth of Patchy Wi-Fi** – Corridors rearrange themselves every time someone checks social media. A rumor says that chanting the hallway's name out loud appeases the Minotaur's router.
2. **The Sarcastic Swamp** – Every vine gives snarky feedback; the mud has opinions on indentation, and frogs insist on unit tests before granting passage.
3. **The Undersea World of Unexpected Meetings** – Bubble-bound boardrooms drift by, and every eel demands status updates in triplicate.
