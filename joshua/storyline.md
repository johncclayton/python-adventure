# Joshua's Protein-Powered Slam Saga

## Purpose
Turn Joshua into the galaxy's most shredded and sharp-witted point guard by fusing ridiculous basketball quests with Python wizardry, ensuring every solved puzzle pumps iron into his trophy cabinet.

## Adventure Story / Problem
Joshua "Quadruple-Scoop" Rivers has been drafted into the Ultra-Mega Protein Pro League, an elite circus run by Coach Flexadecimal — a jacked hologram with a pitch-black sense of humor, a bottomless protein stash, and an unhealthy obsession with hiding critical instructions behind code riddles. The coach's favorite prank is to stash the clue for the *next* drill inside the solution to the *current* one, often delivered by a flock of sarcastic chickens wearing tiny referee shirts.

Solving each Python quest unwraps a key fragment: coordinates to the next arena, the recipe for the next recovery shake, or the punchline to a joke so dark it requires a spotter. If Joshua misses a puzzle, the chickens refuse to share the clue, the blender locks itself, and Coach Flexadecimal starts reading protein puns in a menacing whisper.

Joshua must conquer three nutri-arenas to assemble the legendary Golden Shaker Cup dossier:

1. **Broccoli Dunk Bunker** – A subterranean gym where hoops are steamed greens, shot clocks smell faintly of whey, and broody chickens guard macro secrets inside hollowed-out kettlebells.
2. **Swolestice Spin Class Coliseum** – A rotating court powered by stationary bikes pedaled by aspiring fitness influencers; each lap reveals a clue etched in chia pudding, but only after Joshua debugs the coach's latest morbid punchline.
3. **Zero-Gravity Smoothie Nebula** – An orbiting blender arena where basketballs float, protein powder forms constellations, and every solved function cues a chicken to cackle out the coordinates for the next drill.

As Joshua fights through burpees, bugs, and poultry pranks, the Protein Council chants, "Refactor! Rehydrate! Reinforce your glutes!" Completing each Python challenge is the only way to unlock the next twisted note from Coach Flexadecimal, slam-dunk in orbit, and finally clutch the world champion crown (which doubles as a shaker lid and emergency chicken coop).
